Review criteria

  • Can each user identify the next action without explanation?
  • Is device state visible before and after an action?
  • Are restrictions understandable without exposing settings?
  • Can the administrator tell exactly what an approval permits?

The restriction explanation and request-access flow are hypotheses for follow-up validation, not findings already established by the interviews.
